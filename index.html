    Data sources and attribution

    Gene-expression data displayed on this website are from the Allen Human Brain Atlas, Allen Institute for Brain Science: microarray measurements of gene expression across the adult human brain.

    Hawrylycz MJ, Lein ES, Guillozet-Bongaarts AL, et al. (2012). An anatomically comprehensive atlas of the adult human transcriptome. Nature, 489, 391–399. doi:10.1038/nature11405

    © 2010 Allen Institute for Brain Science. Allen Human Brain Atlas. Available from: human.brain-map.org

    Single-nucleus cell types (the "cells" view): Siletti K, et al. Transcriptomic diversity of cell types across the adult human brain. Science 382, eadd7046 (2023). Data: Human Brain Cell Atlas v1.0 via CELLxGENE Census (release 2025-01-30), CC BY 4.0; aggregated here per dissection and supercluster.

    Protein Atlas brain clusters (the "Protein Atlas clusters" rows): cluster names and expression profiles from the Human Protein Atlas single cell type data (brain neurons and non-neurons, based on Siletti et al. 2023), proteinatlas.org, CC BY-SA 4.0; nuclei assigned to them here by marker-gene correlation.

    Subcellular location images and annotations (the gene menu and the "S" view) are retrieved live from the Human Protein Atlas, proteinatlas.org (Thul PJ, et al. A subcellular map of the human proteome. Science 356, eaal3321 (2017)), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
